SpaceX prices the largest stock market debut in history

SpaceX set the terms of its initial public offering on Wednesday, and the numbers are staggering. The deal values the company at a valuation of $1.75 trillion, far ahead of any prior listing.

The rocket and satellite company will raise $75 billion in the offering, according to the filing. It is offering 556.6 million shares to investors.

The stock will be listed on the Nasdaq Global Select Market. The company will trade under the ticker symbol SPCX. Shares are scheduled to begin trading on June 12, 2026.

Bankers involved in the deal described demand as unprecedented, with order books covered within hours of opening.
