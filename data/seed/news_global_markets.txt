GLOBAL MARKETS POST
SpaceX IPO set for June 12 debut

SpaceX will complete the largest initial public offering ever recorded next week. Sources close to the syndicate said the deal carries a valuation of $1.77 trillion.

The company plans to raise $75 billion in gross proceeds. Shares are expected to begin trading on June 12, 2026.

After the deal, the free float will be just 4.3 percent of shares outstanding, a thin slice that some investors warn could amplify price swings.

Index providers said they would announce eligibility decisions after the first week of trading.
