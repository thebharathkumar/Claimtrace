ORBIT BUSINESS REVIEW
What the SpaceX listing means for investors

The most anticipated stock offering of the decade is here. The deal gives SpaceX a valuation of $1.75 trillion at the offer.

The offering has been priced at $134.75 per share. The underwriters have set aside a retail allocation of 10 percent of the deal for individual investors.

The company disclosed revenue of $14.2 billion for its latest fiscal year. Growth has been driven by the Starlink satellite internet business, which now accounts for the majority of sales.

Fund managers say the listing will force a rebalancing of major indexes once the stock becomes eligible for inclusion.
