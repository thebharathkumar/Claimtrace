MARKET DAILY
Musk's SpaceX heads for a record float

SpaceX is going public at last. The long awaited listing values the company at a $1.77 trillion valuation, according to people familiar with the deal.

The company is seeking to raise as much as $80 billion from investors. The offering covers 555.6 million shares of common stock.

Retail brokerages said interest from individual investors has been intense ahead of the debut. Analysts cautioned that early trading could be volatile given the size of the deal.
