SPACE EXPLORATION TECHNOLOGIES CORP.
PROSPECTUS EXCERPT (SEED DATA FOR DEMONSTRATION)

This is the initial public offering of common stock of Space Exploration Technologies Corp. Our common stock has been approved for listing on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker symbol SPCX.

We are offering 556.6 million shares of our common stock. The offering has been priced at $134.75 per share. The company expects to raise $75 billion in gross proceeds from the sale of shares.

The offering implies a valuation of $1.75 trillion for the company. Shares are expected to begin trading on June 12, 2026.

Following the offering, the free float will represent approximately 4.3 percent of shares outstanding. The underwriters have reserved a retail allocation of 10 percent of the shares in this offering.

For the year ended December 31, 2025, we recorded revenue of $14.2 billion. Over the same period we recorded a net loss of $1.3 billion.
