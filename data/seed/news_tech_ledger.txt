TECH LEDGER
The numbers behind the SpaceX offering

SpaceX is about to test how much capital public markets can absorb in one deal. The company is raising $80 billion in its initial public offering, the ledger of record-setting figures shows.

The company is offering 556.6 million shares in the deal. The stock will be listed on the Nasdaq exchange. It will trade under the ticker symbol SPCX.

The company most recently reported a net loss of $1.3 billion, reflecting continued heavy investment in the Starship program.

Engineers and early employees are expected to see significant liquidity for the first time in the company's two decade history.
